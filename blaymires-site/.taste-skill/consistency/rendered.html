Questions about a callout

Do you cover emergencies 24 hours a day?

Yes. The phone is answered day and night, 365 days a year, including bank holidays.

How quickly can someone get to me?

Across LS11 we aim to be with you within the hour. Further out in Leeds, we give you a realistic time on the phone.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes. Every gas job is done by a Gas Safe registered engineer, and we are fully insured.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. You get the price first, and if the job changes once we open it up, we stop and ask you.

Can I send a photo of the problem?

Yes. A photo of the boiler display or the leak helps us bring the right parts the first time.

Do you work with landlords and letting agents?

Yes. We run annual services and gas safety checks for rental properties across Leeds.
