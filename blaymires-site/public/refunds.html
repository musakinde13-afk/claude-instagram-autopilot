Cancellations and refunds

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Your right to cancel

If you agree work with us at your home or by phone, email or this website, you normally have 14 days from the day the contract is made to cancel it for any reason. To cancel, tell us clearly by phone on 0113 270 2616, by email at %%BUSINESS_EMAIL%%, or by post to Office 010, Upper Wortley Business Centre, 127 Upper Wortley Road, Leeds LS12 4JG. You can use the model cancellation form below, but you don't have to.

If you ask us to start within the 14 days

You can ask us to start work before the 14 days are up. If you then cancel, you pay for the work done up to the point you told us. If the work is finished within the 14 days at your request, and you have acknowledged that you lose your right to cancel once it is finished, you can no longer cancel.

Urgent repairs

If you asked us to visit to carry out urgent repairs or maintenance, such as a leak or a boiler breakdown, you cannot cancel those urgent repairs. Your right to cancel still applies to any extra work or goods we supply on that visit that you did not ask for, or that go beyond what the urgent repair needed.

Refunds

If you cancel within your rights, we will refund what you have paid, minus any amount for work already done at your request, within 14 days of you telling us, using the same payment method you used unless you agree otherwise.

If our work or parts are faulty

If work was not done with reasonable care and skill, we will come back and put it right at no cost to you. If that isn't possible, or we can't do it within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience to you, you are entitled to a price reduction, which may be a full refund. If parts we supplied are faulty, you can reject them within 30 days for a refund. After that, you are entitled to a repair or replacement, and then a refund or price reduction if that doesn't fix it. These rights come from the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and are not affected by anything on this page.
