Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. Blaymires Plumbing & Heating ("we") is the data controller for information you send through this website.

What we collect

When you request a call out we collect your name, phone number, email (if given), address, postcode, a description of the problem, your preferred date and time, and any photo you upload. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records anonymous usage such as pages visited.

Why we use it

To contact you, arrange and carry out the work you asked for, and keep records required for Gas Safe and tax purposes. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract, and our legitimate interest in running the business.

Who sees it

Only Blaymires. Form submissions are processed by Netlify (website host) and stored in Google Workspace (email, calendar and spreadsheet). We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become jobs are deleted after 12 months. Job records are kept for 6 years.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by calling 0113 270 2616. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

We set one cookie preference in your browser. Google Analytics cookies are only set if you click Accept, and you can change this any time with Cookie settings in the footer.
