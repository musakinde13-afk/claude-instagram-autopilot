What we collect

When you request a call out: your name, phone number, address and postcode, a description of the problem, how urgent it is, your preferred date and time slot, and, only if you choose to give them, your email address and a photo of the problem. Please keep people out of any photo you send.

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site, such as pages visited and the type of device. See our cookie policy.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

We use your call out details to contact you, quote for and carry out the work you ask for. The lawful basis is that this is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract with you, and to perform that contract. We keep job records because the law requires us to (for example tax records), which is a legal obligation. We use analytics only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not use your details for marketing, sell them, or make decisions about you by automated means.

Who we share it with

We use service providers who process information on our behalf and only on our instructions: Netlify, Inc. (hosts this website and receives form submissions), Google (email, calendar and spreadsheets used to manage bookings, and Google Analytics if you consent). These providers may process data in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK–US data bridge or the UK's international data transfer agreement or addendum. We may also share information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

If a request does not lead to any work, we delete it within 12 months. If we do work for you, we keep the job record for 6 years after the end of the financial year it relates to, to meet tax and legal requirements, and then delete it.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to data portability where it applies. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To use any of these rights, contact us as above. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Do you have to give us your details?

No, but without your name, phone number and address we cannot arrange a call out.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
