Cookie policy

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. This site only sets the ones below, and only sets analytics cookies if you click Accept analytics.

Strictly necessary

consent, stored in your browser's local storage: remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics so we don't ask again. It stays until you clear your browser data. This does not need your consent because the site needs it to respect your choice.

Analytics (only with your consent)

_ga and _ga_<ID>, set by Google Analytics: tell us how many people visit and which pages they use, so we can improve the site. They last up to 2 years. Google's own privacy information is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Changing your mind

Use Cookie settings, also in the footer of any page to accept or reject analytics at any time. If you reject after accepting, we delete the analytics cookies on this site. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
