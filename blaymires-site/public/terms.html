Requests

Sending a call out request does not confirm an appointment. We will call you to agree a time and, where possible, a price before any work starts.

Call out charges

A call out charge applies to attendance and diagnosis and will be confirmed when we call you back. Parts and further labour are quoted separately.

Payment

Payment is due on completion unless agreed otherwise in writing. Receipts are available on request.

Guarantees

Our workmanship is guaranteed for 12 months. Manufacturer warranties on boilers and parts apply on their own terms, and we will register new boilers with the manufacturer for you.

Cancellations

Please give us as much notice as you can if you need to cancel. Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations you have 14 days to cancel work booked off-premises, unless you asked us to start sooner.

Liability

Nothing in these terms limits your statutory rights as a consumer.
