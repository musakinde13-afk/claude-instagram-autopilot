Privacy policy

Blaymires Plumbing & Heating only collects the information needed to quote and carry out work at your property: your name, phone number, postcode and a description of the issue. Photos you send are used only to diagnose the fault.

Your details are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing. Where a manufacturer warranty or Building Regulations notification is required, the minimum necessary information is passed to that body on your behalf.

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to complete the job and to honour any warranty, and are then removed. You may ask us to delete your details at any time by calling 07961 863549.
